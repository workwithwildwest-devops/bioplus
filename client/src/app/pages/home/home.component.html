Why Choose BioPlus

At BioPlus Laboratory, we provide cutting-edge diagnostic solutions with a commitment to accuracy, reliability, and fast results.

HAEMATOLOGY

Our hematology services provide precise analysis of blood conditions, helping in the diagnosis and monitoring of various disorders. Using advanced technology, we ensure accurate results for conditions such as anemia, infections, and blood cancers.

MICROBIOLOGY

Our microbiology services specialize in identifying pathogens and microorganisms that cause infections, from bacteria to fungi and viruses. With advanced testing methods, we help diagnose a wide range of infectious diseases and guide effective treatment options.

Clinical Pathology

Our clinical pathology services offer detailed analysis of bodily fluids, such as blood and urine, to diagnose and monitor various health conditions. With advanced techniques and expert interpretation, we provide crucial insights for the detection of diseases and ongoing patient care.

Specialized Genetic Testing

Our staff of trained Phlebotomists are experienced in performing blood draws and specialized genetic testing to provide comprehensive health insights and personalized treatment recommendations.
