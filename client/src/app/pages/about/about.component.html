Our Story

BioPlus was founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to provide accurate, reliable, and timely laboratory services that help healthcare providers make better decisions for their patients.

Over the years, we have grown from a small local laboratory to a comprehensive diagnostic center equipped with state-of-the-art technology and staffed by experienced professionals.

Today, we serve thousands of patients and healthcare providers, maintaining our commitment to excellence and innovation in medical diagnostics.
