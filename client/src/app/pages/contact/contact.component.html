Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How long does it take to get results?

Most routine tests are completed within 24-48 hours. Complex tests may take 3-5 business days. We offer express services for urgent cases.

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend booking an appointment to ensure minimal wait times and better service.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a valid ID, insurance card (if applicable), doctor's prescription, and any previous test results.

Need Immediate Assistance?

For urgent medical needs or emergency services, call our 24/7 emergency hotline.
